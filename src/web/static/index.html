How StoreSight measures behaviour

Store interest
A passer-by faces the store (face turned to the in-store camera) or stops at the glass for ≥ 0.8 s of a 3 s window. Anyone who walks in counts too.
Entered
Crosses the entrance line, goes ≥ 20 px past it and stays inside 1 s.
Shelf interest
A customer at the shelf, nearly still and not turned away, for 2 s. One episode per visit; returning after 3 s away is a new one.
Interaction
Staff and customer within ~1.6 m, both still, one facing the other, for 2 s. Pauses up to 4 s keep the same session.
